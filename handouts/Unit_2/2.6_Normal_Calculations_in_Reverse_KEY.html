2.6  Normal Calculations in Reverse

Mini Lesson - Unit 2: Analyzing One Variable - DHS Statistics
Teacher Key: answers in red

In 2.5, you knew the value and found the percent. Now you know the percent and find the value.

Example: Heights of adult women: mean 64 inches, SD 3 inches. How tall is a woman at the 90th percentile?

StepsExample: 90th percentile
1. Draw the curve. Shade the area to the left.90% is to the left. The area is .9000.
2. Look inside the table for the area closest to it.The closest area is .8997.
3. Read the z-score from the row and the column.Row 1.2, column .08:  z=1.28z = 1.28
4. Find the value:  x=μ+zσx = \mu + z\sigma64+1.28×3=64+3.84=67.8464 + 1.28 \times 3 = 64 + 3.84 = 67.84

A woman at the 90th percentile is about 67.8 inches tall.

“Top” and “bottom” questions

The bottom 10%area to the left is .10
The top 10%area to the left is 1−.10=.901 - .10 = .90

Example: Below what height are the shortest 10% of women?

Area .1000.  Closest in the table: .1003 at z=−1.28z = -1.28.  64+(−1.28)(3)=64−3.84=60.1664 + (-1.28)(3) = 64 - 3.84 = 60.16.  About 60.2 inches.

Gotcha
The table gives areas to the left. For “top” questions, subtract from 1 before you look in the table.

2. A test has scores with a normal distribution. The mean is 500 and the SD is 100.
  1. What score is at the 90th percentile?
    z=1.28z = 1.28.  500+1.28×100=628500 + 1.28 \times 100 = 628
  2. What score is at the 30th percentile?
    z=−0.52z = -0.52.  500+(−0.52)(100)=448500 + (-0.52)(100) = 448
3. A phone battery lasts a mean of 20 hours with an SD of 2 hours. The times have a normal distribution. The company replaces any battery in the bottom 10%. Below what time does a battery get replaced?
Area .10:  z=−1.28z = -1.28.  20+(−1.28)(2)=20−2.56=17.4420 + (-1.28)(2) = 20 - 2.56 = 17.44.  About 17.4 hours.
4. Apples have a mean weight of 150 grams and an SD of 10 grams. The weights have a normal distribution. The heaviest 15% are sold as “premium.” How heavy must an apple be to be premium?
Top 15%, so the area to the left is 1−.15=.851 - .15 = .85.  Closest: .8508 at z=1.04z = 1.04.  150+1.04×10=160.4150 + 1.04 \times 10 = 160.4 grams.
5. Use the test from problem 2 (mean 500, SD 100). One question goes forward and one goes in reverse.
  1. What percentile is a score of 640?
    z=640−500100=1.4z = \frac{640 - 500}{100} = 1.4.  Table: .9192.  About the 92nd percentile.
  2. What score is at the 75th percentile?

6. Challenge. The heights of a type of plant have a normal distribution with a mean of 30 cm. 97.5% of the plants are shorter than 39.8 cm. Find the SD.
97.5th percentile: z=1.96z = 1.96.  30+1.96σ=39.830 + 1.96\sigma = 39.8, so 1.96σ=9.81.96\sigma = 9.8 and σ=9.8÷1.96=5\sigma = 9.8 \div 1.96 = 5 cm.
