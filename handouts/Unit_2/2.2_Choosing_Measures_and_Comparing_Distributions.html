Which measures should you use? It depends on the shape of the data.

Shape of the dataCenterSpread
Symmetric, no outliersmeanstandard deviation
Skewed, or has outliersmedianIQR
Why?
An outlier pulls the mean and the SD. It does not pull the median and the IQR much.

Example: 2, 3, 3, 4, 5, 40

MeanSDMedianIQR
With 409.515.03.52
Without 403.41.132

One value moved the mean by 6.1 and the SD by about 14. The median moved 0.5. The IQR did not move.

Comparing two distributions. Talk about all four of these. Use numbers and the context.

Shape:symmetric or skewed?
Center:which group is higher? (median or mean)
Spread:which group is more spread out? (IQR or SD)
Outliers:any values far from the rest?
Shape9th grade is symmetric. 12th grade is skewed right.
CenterThe median for 9th grade (8 hours) is greater than for 12th grade (6 hours). 9th graders usually sleep about 2 hours more.
SpreadBoth groups have an IQR of 2 hours. The spreads are about the same.
OutliersOne 12th grader slept 12 hours. That is an outlier (the upper fence is 10).

12th grade has an outlier, so we compare with the median and the IQR.
