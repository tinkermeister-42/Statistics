4.3  Residuals

Mini Lesson - Unit 4: Scatterplots - DHS Statistics
Teacher Key: answers in red

A line of best fit makes predictions. Most real points are a little above or below the line. A residual measures how far off the prediction is.

residual == actual −- predicted =y−ŷ= y - \hat{y}
Positive residual: the point is above the line.     Negative residual: the point is below the line.

Example: The line is ŷ=5x+54\hat{y} = 5x + 54. Two students studied 4 hours.

xxyy (actual)ŷ\hat{y} (predicted)y−ŷy - \hat{y}Meaning
4785(4)+54=745(4) + 54 = 7478−74=478 - 74 = 4scored 4 points more than predicted
4727472−74=−272 - 74 = {-2}scored 2 points less than predicted

The least-squares line. Square every residual and add them: Σ(y−ŷ)2\Sigma(y - \hat{y})^2. The least-squares regression line is the line that makes this sum as small as possible. Calculators and computers find this line for you.

Residual plots. Put xx across the bottom and the residuals up the side.

A: random scatter, no pattern.A line is a good model.
B: a curved pattern.A line is not a good model. The data is curved.

  1. Which weeks have points above the line?  Weeks 1, 3, and 5 (positive residuals)
  2. Is the point for week 2 above or below the line? How do you know?  Below. Its residual is negative.
2. Use the study line ŷ=5x+54\hat{y} = 5x + 54.
  1. A student studied 6 hours and scored 90. Find the residual.
    ŷ=5(6)+54=84\hat{y} = 5(6) + 54 = 84.  Residual =90−84=6= 90 - 84 = 6.
  2. Another student has a residual of −5-5. What does that mean?
    The student scored 5 points less than the line predicted.
3. The used car line is ŷ=−2.5x+30\hat{y} = {-2.5}x + 30 (value in thousands of dollars). A 6-year-old car is worth $14,000. Find the residual and explain it.
ŷ=−2.5(6)+30=15\hat{y} = {-2.5}(6) + 30 = 15.  Residual =14−15=−1= 14 - 15 = {-1}. The car is worth $1,000 less than predicted.
