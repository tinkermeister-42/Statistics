Describing a Scatterplot

A scatterplot shows two numbers for each person or thing. The first number ($x$) goes across. The second number ($y$) goes up.

FeatureAskExample: the study data
DirectionAs $x$ goes up, does $y$ go up (positive) or down (negative)? No pattern at all is no association.Positive. The students who studied more mostly scored higher.
FormDo the dots follow a line (linear) or a curve?Linear. The dots follow a line.
StrengthAre the dots close to a line (strong), a little spread out (moderate), or very spread out (weak)?Strong. The dots are close to the line.
UnusualAny outliers or clusters?None.

The Line of Best Fit

A line of best fit follows the trend of the dots. It goes through the middle of the dots. About the same number of dots are above it as below it.

StepHow it worksExample: the study data
1. Pick two pointsPick two points on the line. They do not have to be dots.$(2, 64)$ and $(6, 84)$
2. Find the slope $m$Subtract the $y$ values. Subtract the $x$ values in the same order. Divide.$m = \dfrac{84 - 64}{6 - 2} = \dfrac{20}{4} = 5$
3. Find the y-intercept $b$The equation is $\hat{y} = mx + b$. Put in $m$ and one point. Solve for $b$.$64 = 5(2) + b$,  so $b = 54$
4. Write the equationPut $m$ and $b$ into $\hat{y} = mx + b$. $\hat{y}$ is read “y-hat.” The hat means predicted.$\hat{y} = 5x + 54$
5. PredictPut the $x$ value into the equation. The line predicts $y$. It does not give the exact $y$.A student studies 5 hours. $\hat{y} = 5(5) + 54 = 79$. The predicted score is 79.
ExtrapolationPredicting far outside the data. It is risky.12 hours: $\hat{y} = 5(12) + 54 = 114$. A test score cannot be more than 100. The data only goes from 1 to 8 hours.

Slope and Intercept in Context

Sentence frameA plant: $\hat{y} = 2.5x + 4$A candle: $\hat{y} = {-2}x + 24$
SlopeFor each additional [$x$ unit], the predicted [$y$] increases (or decreases) by [slope and units].For each additional week, the predicted height increases by 2.5 cm.For each additional hour, the predicted height decreases by 2 cm.
y-interceptWhen [$x$] is 0, the predicted [$y$] is [intercept and units].When $x = 0$ (the day it was planted), the predicted height is 4 cm.When $x = 0$ (a new candle), the predicted height is 24 cm.

Some intercepts have no meaning: if $x$ is a person's height, then $x = 0$ is not possible.

Correlation and Causation

IdeaWhat it meansExample
Correlation coefficient $r$It measures how strong a linear pattern is. $r$ is always from $-1$ to $1$.The candles: $r \approx {-0.98}$. The dots are close to a line that goes down.
Sign of $r$The sign shows the direction. $r > 0$ is positive. $r < 0$ is negative.$r \approx 0.95$ is positive. $r \approx {-0.80}$ is negative.
Size of $r$Close to $1$ or $-1$ is strong. Close to $0$ is weak.$r = {-0.8}$ is stronger than $r = 0.6$. It is closer to $-1$ than $0.6$ is to $1$. The minus sign only tells the direction.
Correlation is not causationA correlation shows that two things change together. It does not prove that one causes the other.Ice cream sales and sunburns have a strong positive correlation. Ice cream does not cause sunburns.
Lurking variableA hidden cause of both things.Hot, sunny weather causes both.

Residuals

IdeaWhat it meansExample: the study line $\hat{y} = 5x + 54$
Residualresidual $=$ actual $-$ predicted $= y - \hat{y}$A student studied 4 hours and scored 78. $\hat{y} = 5(4) + 54 = 74$. Residual $= 78 - 74 = 4$.
Positive residualThe point is above the line.Residual 4: the student scored 4 points more than predicted.
Negative residualThe point is below the line.Another student studied 4 hours and scored 72. Residual $= 72 - 74 = {-2}$. The student scored 2 points less than predicted.

Least Squares and Residual Plots

ToolHow it worksWhat it means
Least-squares regression lineSquare every residual and add them up: $\Sigma(y - \hat{y})^2$. $\Sigma$ means add up.This line makes the sum as small as possible. Calculators and computers find this line for you.
Residual plotPut $x$ across the bottom and the residuals up the side.Random scatter, no pattern: a line is a good model. A curved pattern: a line is not a good model. The data is curved.
