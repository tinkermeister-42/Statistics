Is a lottery ticket worth buying? Is a protection plan worth the money? The expected value of the payoff tells you what happens on average.

Net gain $=$ what you win $-$ what you paid
Expected payoff: let $X$ = the net gain. Then $E(X) = \Sigma\, x \cdot P(x)$.

Example 1: a lottery ticket. A ticket costs \$2. 1 in 1,000 tickets wins \$500. 1 in 50 tickets wins \$10. The rest win nothing.

OutcomeNet gain $x$$P(x)$$x \cdot P(x)$
Win \$500$500 - 2 = 498$0.0010.498
Win \$10$10 - 2 = 8$0.020.16
Win nothing$-2$0.979$-1.958$
$E(X) = \Sigma\, x \cdot P(x)$$-1.30$

On average, a buyer loses \$1.30 per ticket. The lottery keeps that money.

Example 2: a protection plan. A phone costs \$300 to replace. The chance it breaks this year is 0.10. A protection plan costs \$40 for the year.

Expected cost with no plan:$0.10 \cdot 300 = \$30$
Cost of the plan:\$40

On average, the plan costs \$10 more than paying for a broken phone yourself. That is how the company makes money.

But: the plan protects you from a sudden \$300 bill. Expected value is one part of a decision. Risk is another part: could you pay the big bill if it came? If not, the plan might be worth it to you.

3. At a carnival, you pay \$2 and draw one card from a deck. An ace wins \$10. A face card (J, Q, K) wins \$5. Anything else wins nothing.
  1. Find the probability of each outcome.  {{a}}Ace $\frac{4}{52}$, face card $\frac{12}{52}$, other $\frac{36}{52}${{/a}}
  2. Find the expected payoff. Should you expect to win money?
    {{a}}$8 \cdot \frac{4}{52} + 3 \cdot \frac{12}{52} + (-2) \cdot \frac{36}{52} = \frac{32 + 36 - 72}{52} = -\frac{4}{52} \approx -\$0.08$
    No. You lose about 8 cents a game on average.{{/a}}
4. A laptop repair costs \$400. The chance a laptop needs repair in its first year is 0.15. A one-year warranty (a protection plan) costs \$75.
  1. Find the expected repair cost without the warranty.  {{a}}$0.15 \cdot 400 = \$60${{/a}}
  2. Which is cheaper on average: buying the warranty or not?  {{a}}Not buying it (\$60 is less than \$75).{{/a}}
  3. Give a reason someone might still buy the warranty.
    {{a}}They could not afford a sudden \$400 bill. The warranty removes that risk.{{/a}}
5. The store sells 1,000 of these warranties. About how much money does the store expect to make? Explain.
{{a}}It collects $1{,}000 \cdot 75 = \$75{,}000$. About 150 laptops need repair: $150 \cdot 400 = \$60{,}000$. It expects to make about \$15,000.{{/a}}
