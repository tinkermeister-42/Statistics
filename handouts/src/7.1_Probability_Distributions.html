This table is the probability distribution of $X$. It lists every value of $X$ and its probability.

$P(x)$ means the probability that $X$ equals $x$. Capital $X$ is the random variable. Small $x$ is one of its values.

{{fig Unit_7/7.1_two_coins w=1.93in}}
A valid probability distribution:
1. Every probability is from 0 to 1.    2. The probabilities add to 1:  $\Sigma P(x) = 1$

Check: $\frac{1}{4} + \frac{1}{2} + \frac{1}{4} = 1$.  Valid.

Using a distribution. Add the probabilities of the values you want.

$P(X \ge 1) = P(1) + P(2) = \frac{1}{2} + \frac{1}{4} = \frac{3}{4}$    ($\ge$ means “greater than or equal to”)

Theoretical or empirical?

TheoreticalFrom reasoning about the chance process (equally likely outcomes, or the multiplication rule), like the coin flips above.
EmpiricalFrom data. Divide each count by the total.
50 families: 0 cars: 5,  1 car: 20,  2 cars: 18,  3 cars: 7
$P(0) = \frac{5}{50} = 0.10$,  $P(1) = 0.40$,  $P(2) = 0.36$,  $P(3) = 0.14$
