Unit 4 Review

8 candles of the same kind burned for different numbers of hours. Then each candle was measured.

Hours burned (\(x\)) 1 2 3 4 5 6 7 8
Height in cm (\(y\)) 23 19 18 17 13 13 9 8

Every tool from Unit 4 tells you something about these candles. A scatterplot shows the pattern. A line makes predictions. The slope and the intercept tell what the line means. A residual shows how far off a prediction is.

This review uses all of them. It has every kind of problem on the Unit 4 quiz, with more of each.

scatterplot, line of best fit, y-hat, extrapolation, slope, y-intercept, correlation coefficient, causation, lurking variable, residual, least-squares regression line, residual plot

4.1 - Describe and Fit

A scatterplot puts one dot on the graph for each candle. A line of best fit goes through the middle of the dots.

The pattern is negative, linear, and strong.

Two points on the line are \((2, 20)\) and \((7, 10)\).

\[ m = \frac{10 - 20}{7 - 2} = \frac{-10}{5} = -2 \]

\[ 20 = -2(2) + b \quad \Rightarrow \quad b = 24 \]

\[ \hat{y} = -2x + 24 \]

A candle that burns 4.5 hours: \(\hat{y} = -2(4.5) + 24 = 15\) cm.

A candle that burns 15 hours: \(\hat{y} = -2(15) + 24 = -6\) cm. A height cannot be negative. The data only goes from 1 to 8 hours.

4.2 - Explain the Line

Meaning
Slope (\(-2\)) For each additional hour, the predicted height decreases by 2 cm.
y-intercept (24) When \(x = 0\) (a new candle), the predicted height is 24 cm.
\(r\) \(r\) is always from \(-1\) to \(1\). The dots are close to a line that goes down, so \(r\) is close to \(-1\) (\(r \approx -0.98\)).

When more umbrellas are sold, there are more car accidents. Umbrellas do not cause accidents. Rain causes both. Rain is a lurking variable.

4.3 - Find a Residual

\[ \text{residual} = \text{actual} - \text{predicted} = y - \hat{y} \]

  • Positive residual: the point is above the line.
  • Negative residual: the point is below the line.

The candle that burned 4 hours is 17 cm.

\[ \hat{y} = -2(4) + 24 = 16 \]

\[ \text{residual} = 17 - 16 = 1 \]

The residual is positive, so the dot is above the line. The candle is 1 cm taller than predicted.

The least-squares regression line makes \(\Sigma(y - \hat{y})^2\) as small as possible.

The residual plot shows no pattern. A line is a good model.

Test-Style Questions

A test asks the same questions with more words. Read slowly. Choose the best answer.

  1. The scatterplot in Problem 1 shows the number of hours 12 students practiced typing last week and their typing speeds, in words per minute. Which equation best represents a line of best fit for the data?

    A. \(\hat{y} = -3x + 20\)
    B. \(\hat{y} = 3x + 20\)
    C. \(\hat{y} = 20x + 3\)
    D. \(\hat{y} = 3x + 40\)

  2. Mia used the line of best fit \(\hat{y} = 1.5x + 10\) to predict the battery percent, \(y\), of her phone after it charges for \(x\) minutes. Her data included charging times from 5 to 55 minutes. Which prediction is least likely to be reliable?

    A. the battery percent after 10 minutes of charging
    B. the battery percent after 30 minutes of charging
    C. the battery percent after 50 minutes of charging
    D. the battery percent after 75 minutes of charging

  3. A pet store owner found that the equation \(\hat{y} = 2.5x + 20\) can be used to predict the price \(y\), in dollars, of a fish tank that holds \(x\) gallons of water. Which statement best describes the meaning of the slope of the line?

    A. Every fish tank costs \(\$2.50\).
    B. For each additional gallon a tank holds, the predicted price increases by \(\$2.50\).
    C. For each additional dollar in price, a tank holds 2.5 more gallons.
    D. A tank that holds 0 gallons is predicted to cost \(\$2.50\).

  4. A study of 200 students found a strong positive correlation between the number of hours students practice a musical instrument each week and their math grades. Which conclusion is best supported by the study?

    A. Practicing a musical instrument causes students to earn higher math grades.
    B. Earning higher math grades causes students to practice a musical instrument more.
    C. Students who practice more tend to have higher math grades, but the study does not show that one causes the other.
    D. There is no relationship between practicing a musical instrument and math grades.

  5. A bus company uses the line of best fit \(\hat{y} = 3x + 12\) to predict the travel time \(y\), in minutes, for a trip of \(x\) miles. A 6-mile trip took 27 minutes. Which statement about the residual for this trip is true?

    A. The residual is 3, and the point is above the line.
    B. The residual is 3, and the point is below the line.
    C. The residual is \(-3\), and the point is above the line.
    D. The residual is \(-3\), and the point is below the line.

  6. A student found a line of best fit for a data set and made a residual plot. The residuals are positive for small and large values of \(x\) and negative for values of \(x\) in the middle. Which conclusion is best supported by the residual plot?

    A. A line is a good model, because some residuals are positive and some are negative.
    B. A line is not a good model, because the residuals show a curved pattern.
    C. A line is a good model, because the residuals form a clear pattern.
    D. A line is not a good model, because the sum of the residuals is 0.
