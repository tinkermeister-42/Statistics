Unit 3 Review

50 students answered two questions:

  • Do you have a library card?
  • Do you read for fun?

A two-way table shows both answers at once. Percents show if the two answers are connected.

3.1 - Count, Then Divide by All

Reads Does not read Total
Card 14 6 20
No card 9 21 30
Total 23 27 50

Each cell counts students who match both labels. 14 students have a card and read for fun.

Each row adds to its total. Each column adds to its total.

\[14 + 6 = 20 \qquad 14 + 9 = 23\]

The venn diagram shows the same counts. 21 students are outside both circles. They are in neither group.

A relative frequency table divides every count by the grand total, 50.

Reads Does not read Total
Card \(\frac{14}{50} = 28\%\) \(\frac{6}{50} = 12\%\) \(\frac{20}{50} = 40\%\)
No card \(\frac{9}{50} = 18\%\) \(\frac{21}{50} = 42\%\) \(\frac{30}{50} = 60\%\)
Total \(\frac{23}{50} = 46\%\) \(\frac{27}{50} = 54\%\) 100%

28% of all the students have a card and read for fun. The whole table adds to 100%.

3.2 - Divide by One Group

Divide by Example
Marginal distribution the grand total Reads: \(\frac{23}{50} = 46\%\). Does not read: \(\frac{27}{50} = 54\%\).
Conditional distribution that group’s total Card: \(\frac{14}{20} = 70\%\) read. No card: \(\frac{9}{30} = 30\%\) read.

A segmented bar chart shows the two conditional distributions side by side.

70% and 30% are very different. So having a library card and reading for fun are associated.

“Of the students with a card, what percent read for fun?”

Divide by the card total (20). Do not divide by 50. Do not divide by the reads total (23).

We do not know. An association only says the two things happen together. Maybe students who already like to read get a card.

3.1 - Two-Way Tables and Relative Frequency

  1. 120 students in 9th and 10th grade said which after-school club they are in. Each student is in one club.

    Art Chess Garden Total
    9th grade 18 ? ? 55
    10th grade ? 14 25 ?
    Total 44 ? 40 120

    1. Fill in the missing numbers.

    2. How many students are in the chess club?

    3. How many 9th graders are not in the art club?

    4. Which club has the most 10th graders?

    75 students in 11th and 12th grade said what kind of phone they have.

    Android iPhone Total
    11th grade 16 ? 30
    12th grade ? ? ?
    Total ? 39 75

    1. Fill in the missing numbers.

    2. How many 12th graders have an iPhone?

    3. How many students have an Android phone?

  2. This Venn diagram shows how many of 60 students play video games and how many play chess.

    1. Make a two-way table from the Venn diagram. Use the rows “Games” and “No games” and the columns “Chess” and “No chess.”

    2. Make a relative frequency table. Divide each count by 60. Write percents.

    3. What percent of all the students play chess but not video games?

    4. What percent of all the students play at least one of the two games?

    5. Which statement is supported by the relative frequency table?

      A. 9% of the students play both video games and chess.
      B. 25% of the students play chess.
      C. 40% of the students play neither game.
      D. 10% of the students play video games but not chess.

    80 students were asked if they ride a bike and if they skateboard. 46 ride a bike. 22 skateboard. 14 do both.

    1. Make a Venn diagram.

    2. Make a two-way table with the rows “Bike” and “No bike” and the columns “Skate” and “No skate.”

    3. How many students do neither?

    4. What percent of all the students ride a bike and skateboard?

3.2 - Marginal and Conditional Distributions

  1. 120 students in 9th and 10th grade chose their favorite kind of game.

    Board Video Card Total
    9th grade 10 22 8 40
    10th grade 26 34 20 80
    Total 36 56 28 120

    1. Find the marginal distribution of favorite kind of game.

    2. Find the marginal distribution of grade.

    3. Find the conditional distribution of favorite game for 9th graders.

    4. Find the conditional distribution of favorite game for 10th graders.

    5. Find the conditional distribution of grade for the students who chose card games.

    Is each statement a marginal or a conditional percent?

    1. About 47% of all the students chose video games.

    2. Of the 10th graders, 25% chose card games.

    3. 30% of the students chose board games.

    4. 55% of 9th graders chose video games.

  2. A class grew 60 bean plants. Some plants got plant food. The class recorded which plants grew flowers.

    Flowers No flowers Total
    Plant food 30 10 40
    No plant food 9 11 20
    Total 39 21 60

    1. What percent of the plants with plant food grew flowers?

    2. What percent of the plants without plant food grew flowers?

    3. Is plant food associated with growing flowers? Explain.

    4. What percent of the plants with flowers got plant food?

    5. Why are the answers to (a) and (d) different?

    6. Of the plants with no flowers, what percent got no plant food? Which fraction gives the answer?

      A. \(\frac{11}{20}\)
      B. \(\frac{11}{21}\)
      C. \(\frac{11}{60}\)
      D. \(\frac{21}{60}\)

    130 students in 9th and 10th grade said if they write with a pen or a pencil.

    Pen Pencil Total
    9th grade 15 35 50
    10th grade 24 56 80
    Total 39 91 130

    1. What percent of 9th graders use a pen?

    2. What percent of 10th graders use a pen?

    3. Is grade associated with using a pen or a pencil? Explain.

Test-Style Questions

A test asks the same questions with more words. Read slowly. Choose the best answer.

  1. A survey asked 90 students in 11th and 12th grade where they like to watch movies: at a theater or at home. Each student chose one place. The two-way table shows the results, but some of the values are missing.

    Theater Home Total
    11th grade 12 ? 40
    12th grade ? ? ?
    Total 30 ? 90

    Based on the table, how many 12th graders like to watch movies at home?

    A. 28
    B. 32
    C. 50
    D. 60

  2. A school surveyed 200 students about their phones. The two-way table shows whether each phone has a case and whether its screen is cracked.

    Cracked Not cracked Total
    Case 14 106 120
    No case 36 44 80
    Total 50 150 200

    What percent of all the students surveyed have a phone with a case and a cracked screen?

    A. 7%
    B. about 12%
    C. 25%
    D. 28%

  3. A music teacher asked 150 students in 11th and 12th grade which instrument they would most like to learn. The results are shown in the two-way table.

    Guitar Piano Drums Total
    11th 30 18 12 60
    12th 36 45 9 90
    Total 66 63 21 150

    Based on the table, what percent of the 12th graders surveyed want to learn piano?

    A. 30%
    B. 42%
    C. 50%
    D. about 71%

  4. A survey asked students in 9th grade and 12th grade if they like to study with music or in silence. The segmented bar chart shows the conditional distribution of study choice for each grade.

    Which statement is supported by the segmented bar chart?

    A. There is no association, because each bar adds up to 100%.
    B. Grade and study choice are associated, because the percent of students who study with music is different in the two grades.
    C. More 9th graders than 12th graders were surveyed.
    D. Most 12th graders like to study with music.
